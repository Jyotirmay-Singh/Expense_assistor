Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 12, 2026

Privacy at a Glance

We collect only the data needed to run your expense tracker.
Your financial data is never sold to third parties.
You can request export or deletion of your data at any time.
We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit and at rest.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Name and email address provided during account registration.
  • Authentication credentials (passwords are stored as one-way hashes — never in plain text).
  • Communication records if you contact our support team.

Financial Information

  • Expense entries, categories, amounts, dates, and notes that you manually log in the Service.
  • If bank integrations are enabled: read-only transaction data retrieved through authorised third-party connectors. We do not store your banking credentials.

Usage & Technical Information

  • Browser type, operating system, and IP address for security and analytics purposes.
  • Pages visited and features used within the Service (aggregated, not linked to individuals).

2. How We Use Your Data

FinCheck uses the information collected for the following purposes:

  • Providing, maintaining, and improving the FinCheck Service.
  • Authenticating your identity and keeping your account secure.
  • Generating spending summaries, reports, and insights within your account.
  • Sending transactional emails (account confirmation, password reset). We do not send marketing emails without your explicit opt-in.
  • Diagnosing technical issues and monitoring for fraudulent or abusive activity.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We may engage trusted third-party service providers to support the operation of the Service. These providers are permitted to access your data only to perform tasks on our behalf and are contractually obligated not to use it for any other purpose. Categories of providers include:

  • Cloud infrastructure: Hosting and database services for storing application data.
  • Bank / payment connectors: Where enabled, read-only integration partners that surface transaction data in your dashboard. These partners are subject to their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.
  • Analytics: Aggregated, anonymised usage statistics to help us improve the product.
  • Email delivery: Transactional email providers for account and security notifications.

A current list of our sub-processors is available on request at jyotirmaysingh03@gmail.com.

4. Data Security & Retention

FinCheck implements commercially reasonable technical and organisational measures — including TLS encryption in transit, encrypted storage at rest, and access controls — to protect your data from unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure.

We retain your data for as long as your account remains active or as required to provide the Service. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal data within [RETENTION PERIOD, e.g. 30 days], except where retention is required by law.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately at jyotirmaysingh03@gmail.com.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at jyotirmaysingh03@gmail.com.

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten") where no legal basis requires us to retain it.
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Restriction & Objection: Request that we limit or stop certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time without affecting prior processing.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. If you are a California resident, you may have additional rights under the CCPA — contact us at jyotirmaysingh03@gmail.com for details.

Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to FinCheck at jyotirmaysingh03@gmail.com.
